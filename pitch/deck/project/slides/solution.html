Five parts working together. Above, the interface: a web app with the sources and settings, the chat and the slide preview. Below, from left to right: indexing, offline and without a language model: we crawl 41 public websites of UniBE, ARTORG and the Diabetes Center Berne, clean them, split them into passages and embed them with BGE-M3; a new website is one start URL on the Sources page, and Settings updates the index. Then RAG, sitting between the index and Apertus: the search index is stored locally, search works by meaning, with hypothetical passages written in the words of the sources (HyDE), and the best pages are read in full. Then Apertus, the Swiss open model: the 70B, or the lighter 8B as an alternative, chosen in Settings. And export. Next slide: one example at every step.
