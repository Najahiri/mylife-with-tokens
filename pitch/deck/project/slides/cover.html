Hack Apertus

RAGlette: Raclette plus RAG. A Research & Expertise Navigator for Bern: one search across the University of Bern websites, ARTORG and the Diabetes Center Berne. You ask in your own words, for example who works on glucose sensors, and RAGlette finds the people, expertise and infrastructure, even where the websites use different words, and shows the source for every hit. The result can go straight into slides in the Kanton Bern design.
