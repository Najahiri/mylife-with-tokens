The same five parts, now with one example at every exchange. Indexing: the crawler fetches the raw HTML of a public page, here a Diabetes Center Berne article on its four diabetes technology professorships, and the cleaner keeps only the text, which is split into passages and embedded. The chat sends the request to Apertus: a start-up developing wearable glucose sensors. Apertus sends RAG search passages, written the way a matching web page would read. RAG sends back the best pages, among them this DCB article. Apertus writes an item with a quote copied from the page, and the export turns it into a slide in the Kanton Bern design, in the preview and as PDF or PowerPoint. The HTML, the text and the quote are real, from dcberne.com; the passages and the page list are examples.
